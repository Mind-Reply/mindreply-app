# MindReply Ecosystem Alignment

## Public ecosystem

The A11-K marketing, creative, campaign, and SEO work must align with the wider MindReply ecosystem rather than presenting A11-K as an isolated consumer product.

* **MindReply:** private command layer for replies, routes, proof, decisions, and next actions.
* **MindReply Shell:** front layer and private command cockpit.
* **AUREL Proof:** visual standard, trust surface, and premium presentation layer.
* **A11-K Engine:** engine layer for signal intelligence, route logic, decision support, and workflow handling.
* **n8n Workflows:** protected automation layer, referenced only where the public audience needs that context.

## Creative hierarchy

### MindReply

Lead with ownership, control, proof, routes, and decisions. The tone is direct, premium, practical, and operator-focused.

### AUREL Proof

Use AUREL as the quality and presentation standard: polished, composed, evidence-led, and visually credible. Do not describe AUREL as a product feature unless the public product definition confirms that usage.

### A11-K

Position A11-K as the engine and workflow intelligence layer supporting the MindReply system. The earlier voice-first companion concept remains a valid surface direction, but public messaging must not detach it from the MindReply owner-system architecture.

## Public messaging direction

**MindReply:** Your private command layer for replies, routes, proof, and next actions.

**AUREL Proof:** The visual and trust standard behind a premium owner system.

**A11-K:** The engine layer for signal, routes, decisions, and controlled workflows.

These are messaging directions. Feature availability, access, security, privacy, automation, and performance claims require verification before publication.

## Design direction

* MindReply: obsidian, precise, command-oriented, high signal, low noise.
* AUREL: refined, editorial, premium, proof-led, visually controlled.
* A11-K: intelligent, kinetic, structured, route-aware, and technically grounded.
* Keep the systems visually related but clearly separated by role.
* Avoid generic AI imagery, unsupported “autonomous” claims, and fake operational dashboards.

## Campaign architecture

Campaigns should use the following sequence:

1. MindReply establishes the owner-system problem.
2. AUREL demonstrates the quality and trust surface.
3. A11-K explains the engine and workflow intelligence.
4. The verified destination presents the appropriate access or contact action.

## SEO architecture

Use separate intent clusters and pages for:

* MindReply private command layer.
* MindReply Shell and command cockpit.
* AUREL Proof and visual trust standard.
* A11-K Engine and workflow intelligence.
* Owner systems, proof, routes, decisions, and next actions.

Do not create thin pages merely to target each name. Every indexable page needs a distinct user intent, accurate claims, clear ownership, and a verified destination.

## Release rule

The A11-K marketing preview is not automatically the MindReply production site. Production launch requires a verified owner-controlled domain, approved ecosystem copy, working routes, reviewed public claims, and confirmed deployment status.
