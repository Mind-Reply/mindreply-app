# Profit Engine 2026

## Purpose
Operate marketing as a profit-allocation system rather than a traffic or vanity-metric system.

## Operating loop
DEMAND → CAPTURE → QUALIFY → CONVERT → DELIVER → RETAIN → EXPAND → MEASURE PROFIT

## Required economics
Revenue, contribution margin, fully loaded CAC, LTV/expected customer value, CAC payback, conversion, retention/expansion, delivery cost, attribution evidence and cash outcome.

## Growth mechanisms
Capture existing demand, recover missed opportunities, improve offer architecture, build retention/expansion/referral loops, measure fatigue and overlap, and reallocate capital toward proven contribution.

## Release controls
No automatic paid-media spend, irreversible pricing change, live billing change or external commitment without owner approval.

## Success condition
A campaign is successful only when incremental economic value is sufficiently evidenced after relevant costs.
