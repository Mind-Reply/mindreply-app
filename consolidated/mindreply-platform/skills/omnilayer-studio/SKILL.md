# OmniLayer Studio

Purpose: coordinate versioned media generation, market research, diagnostics, and research synthesis with provenance and explicit approval checkpoints.

Inputs may include a primary asset/query, audience, tone, constraints, diagnostics token, research scope and approval flags.

Layers: media generation, market research, web diagnostics, and research synthesis.

Diagnostics run only with explicit consent and temporary credentials. Never store credentials. Claims must preserve provenance.

Orchestration versions artifacts, records invocation metadata, continues independent layers after failures, and exposes approval checkpoints.

No artifact, source, database, live deployment, or diagnostic result is claimed unless produced and verified.
