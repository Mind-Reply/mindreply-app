# A11pro Layer Polish & Activation — 2026-09

## Objective
Turn the research and profit strategy into a controlled operating system that is useful immediately, measurable at every layer, visually coherent, secure by default, and never allowed to imply activity that has not been verified.

Operating rule:
VERIFY → PROVE → PROTECT → PROPOSE → OWNER APPROVE → EXECUTE → VERIFY → RECORD

No hidden background process, automatic spend, irreversible commercial change, or external commitment is implied by this design.

## Layer 0 — Authority and control
One owner identity, one approval vocabulary, one decision queue. Every material action has status PROPOSED, APPROVED, EXECUTING, VERIFIED, BLOCKED, FAILED or UNVERIFIED. Recommendation and execution are separate.

## Layer 1 — Evidence and truth
Evidence is a first-class object. Every metric has source, period, definition, freshness and confidence. Live claims require a current verification event.

## Layer 2 — Data foundation
Use one canonical vocabulary; preserve raw events separately from derived metrics; never overwrite historical commercial facts.

## Layer 3 — Profit economics
Use contribution economics, variable delivery/usage costs, contribution profit, margin, CAC, payback, retention, expansion and expected value.

## Layer 4 — Demand and discovery
Connect search, answer-based discovery, referrals and direct demand; measure qualified outcomes rather than mentions.

## Layer 5 — Evaluation and conversion
Problem → proof → fit → offer → decision → payment/commitment.

## Layer 6 — Revenue recovery and lifecycle
Recover abandoned enquiry, checkout, missed contact, dormant customer, renewal risk, expansion and referral opportunities.

## Layer 7 — Experimentation and measurement
Separate optimization telemetry from causal proof; use attribution, incrementality and aggregate modeling appropriately.

## Layer 8 — Capital allocation
Decisions: INCREASE / MAINTAIN / TEST / REPAIR / PAUSE / RETIRE. Material spend changes require owner approval.

## Layer 9 — Margin protection
Protect contribution margin and expose cost drift.

## Layer 10 — Retention and expansion
Separate renewal, expansion and customer contribution from acquisition economics.

## Layer 11 — Executive cockpit
Answer where money is made, where money leaks, what changed, what requires a decision and what has been proven.

## Layer 12 — Security and access
Separate public marketing, customer data, credentials and internal economics into distinct trust zones.

## Layer 13 — Design system and experience
Premium operational clarity, strong typography, restrained motion, clear status, evidence provenance and mobile decision capability.

## Layer 14 — Deployment and verification
SOURCE → BUILD → DEPLOY → HEALTH → FUNCTION → EVIDENCE → OWNER RECORD.

## Layer 15 — Operating cadence
MANUAL, EVENT-DRIVEN or SCHEDULED only when explicitly enabled. Every mode has scope, trigger, permissions, timeout, output, verification and stop condition.

## Definition of done
A layer is ACTIVE only when its data contract, control boundary, user action, verification method, evidence record and failure/rollback path exist. Otherwise it is DESIGN, READY, BLOCKED or UNVERIFIED.
