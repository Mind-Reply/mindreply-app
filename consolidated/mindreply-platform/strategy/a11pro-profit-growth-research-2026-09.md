# A11pro Profit Growth Research Integration — 2026-09

## Research signals
B2B discovery increasingly spans search and conversational/answer-based discovery. Buyers increasingly expect transparent pricing logic, ROI evidence, implementation detail, security evidence and material for internal approval.

## Operating changes
1. Answer Presence tracks category, problem and comparison queries, mentions, factual accuracy, proof availability and source coverage.
2. Evaluation Pack exposes outcome, scope, implementation, pricing logic, economics, security evidence, proof, risks and next action.
3. Measurement Triad uses attribution, incrementality and aggregate modeling appropriately.
4. Profit unit is OFFER × CUSTOMER SEGMENT × SOURCE × PERIOD.
5. Capital allocation uses increase, maintain, test, repair, pause, retire with owner approval.
6. Lifecycle recovery covers abandoned enquiry, checkout, missed contact, dormant customer, renewal, expansion and referral.
7. Offer architecture supports diagnostic, intervention, implementation, managed layer, recurring support and expansion modules.
8. Margin Guard monitors contribution margin and cost drift.
9. Evidence-first conversion uses verified results and reproducible proof.
10. Decision latency follows SIGNAL → DIAGNOSIS → PROPOSED ACTION → OWNER APPROVAL → EXECUTION → VERIFICATION → LEDGER.

## Core surfaces
Profit Radar; Demand & Answer Presence; Conversion Observatory; Revenue Recovery; Retention/Expansion; Experiment Lab; Capital Allocation; Evidence Ledger; Margin Guard; Executive Decision Queue.

No automated background process is required by this specification.
