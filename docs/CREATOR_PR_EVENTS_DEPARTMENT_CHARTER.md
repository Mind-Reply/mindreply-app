# Creator, PR & Events Department Charter

Status: READY — role and operating model definition. This document does not evidence an appointment, employment relationship, external partnership or completed campaign.

## Department purpose

Build and operate a disciplined commercial relationship layer across creators, talent, recruitment, media, PR, events, venues, production suppliers and strategic partners.

The department connects four capabilities:

- creator and talent development
- recruitment and staffing
- PR and media relations
- end-to-end event and partnership management

## Chief of Department

**Operating title:** Chief, Creator • Recruitment • PR & Events.

The Chief is accountable for:

1. Department priorities and pipeline hygiene.
2. Quality of creator, candidate, media and partner qualification.
3. Campaign, event and relationship coordination.
4. Proposal readiness and execution handoff.
5. Evidence and status integrity.
6. Escalation of commercial, legal, financial, access, reputational and safety decisions.
7. Post-delivery reporting and relationship retention.

The Chief does not independently create external commitments that require owner approval.

## Team lanes

### Creator & Talent

Source, qualify and coordinate creators and talent by fit, audience, content quality, rights, commercial terms and evidence.

### Recruitment

Translate approved role needs into sourcing, screening, interview, selection and onboarding workflows.

### PR & Media

Develop story angles, press lists, media assets, outreach drafts, response tracking and coverage evidence.

### Events & Partnerships

Manage the full event lifecycle: discovery, partner qualification, venue/vendor sourcing, production planning, guest operations, sponsorship coordination, event-day execution and post-event reporting.

## Partnership operating model

Use explicit relationship states:

PROSPECT → CONTACTED → QUALIFIED → PROPOSAL → AWAITING_APPROVAL → CONFIRMED → IN_EXECUTION → DELIVERED → RETAINED or CLOSED

Only evidence may move an external relationship into CONFIRMED.

## Department scorecard

Track facts rather than vanity metrics:

- qualified creator/talent opportunities
- qualified candidates and recruitment cycle stages
- qualified media opportunities
- outreach sent and response evidence
- confirmed partnerships
- active campaigns
- event pipeline and execution status
- deliverables completed
- rights/permissions completed
- coverage and content evidence
- attendance evidence where applicable
- commercial outcomes where evidenced
- retained relationships
- blocked items and owner decisions required

No target, conversion rate, revenue figure, attendance number or partnership count should be presented as real until supported by evidence.

## Execution gate

Before any external execution, the record must identify:

- accountable owner
- counterpart
- objective and scope
- evidence/source
- stage
- next action
- due date
- budget where relevant
- rights/permissions where relevant
- approval requirements
- evidence location

Before a relationship is marked CONFIRMED, required owner approval and counterpart confirmation must both exist.

## Event-production gate

A confirmed event workflow should include:

DISCOVERY → PARTNER/VENUE QUALIFICATION → SCOPE → BUDGET → APPROVAL → PRODUCTION PLAN → GUEST/CREATOR/MEDIA PLAN → EVENT-DAY CONTROL → EVIDENCE CAPTURE → RECONCILIATION → REPORT → RETENTION

Safety, accessibility, insurance, legal and rights requirements are handled according to the event and jurisdiction; the repository does not treat their existence as automatically satisfied.

## External communications

The department may draft communications and stage outreach. Sending sensitive, legally binding, contractual, financial or commitment-bearing communications requires the applicable owner approval.

## Evidence standard

Every meaningful external claim should be traceable to a source, document, correspondence record, system record, or other durable evidence. Discovery is not confirmation. A proposal is not a partnership. A repository record is not a completed event.
