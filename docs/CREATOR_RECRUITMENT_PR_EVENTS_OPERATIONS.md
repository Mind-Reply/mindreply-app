# Creator, Recruitment, PR & Events Operations

Status: READY — operating contract for the Creator, Recruitment, PR & Events department. This document defines execution boundaries; it does not claim external partnerships, hires, placements, media coverage, event bookings, or live campaigns until evidence exists.

## Department mandate

The department coordinates four connected workstreams:

1. Content creator operations — briefs, creator discovery, qualification, deliverables, rights, publishing calendar and performance evidence.
2. Recruitment — role intake, candidate sourcing, screening, interview stages, offer/approval gates and onboarding handoff.
3. PR management — media/press targets, story angles, press assets, outreach staging, response tracking and coverage evidence.
4. Full event management partnerships — event discovery, venue/vendor/partner qualification, budgets, production plans, guest operations, sponsorships and post-event reporting.

The department is designed as one operating pipeline rather than four disconnected functions. Creator relationships can feed campaigns and events; PR can amplify launches and event moments; recruitment can staff delivery; event partners can become recurring commercial relationships.

## Chief of Department role

**Role definition:** Chief, Creator • Recruitment • PR & Events.

The Chief owns department coordination, prioritisation, quality control, evidence discipline and handoffs across the four workstreams. This is an operating-role definition only; it is not evidence that a person has been appointed, hired or contracted.

Chief responsibilities:

- maintain the department pipeline and priority queue
- approve internal readiness for proposals and execution handoff
- coordinate creator/talent, recruitment, PR, media, venue, production and sponsorship workflows
- ensure every external opportunity has an identified counterpart, objective, owner, next action and evidence location
- maintain partner and relationship records without representing prospects as confirmed partners
- coordinate campaign calendars, event production plans and post-event reporting
- escalate spend, contractual, legal, reputational, access and safety decisions to the required owner
- maintain measurable reporting across delivery, response, conversion, attendance, coverage and commercial outcomes where data exists

## Operating pipeline

DISCOVER → QUALIFY → BRIEF → APPROVE → EXECUTE → EVIDENCE → REPORT → RETAIN

No external commitment is considered executed merely because a task, draft, repository record or proposal exists.

## Partnership lanes

Track partnerships separately so commercial and operational obligations remain explicit:

- Creator / talent
- Media / press
- Agency / production
- Event / venue
- Sponsor / commercial partner
- Technology / platform
- Community / institutional
- Supplier / contractor
- Internal recruitment

A prospect is not a partner until counterpart confirmation and the required owner approval are evidenced.

## Evidence gates

A creator is qualified only when identity, audience/relevance, commercial fit and available evidence have been reviewed.

A recruitment candidate is qualified only when the role requirements and candidate evidence are recorded.

A PR opportunity is qualified only when the publication/contact opportunity and story relevance are evidenced.

An event partnership is qualified only when the event/partner, commercial terms and execution requirements are evidenced.

A partnership becomes committed only after the required owner approval and counterpart confirmation exist.

## Approval boundaries

Owner approval is required before:

- signing or accepting a contract
- committing spend
- publishing on behalf of a third party
- promising deliverables to an external counterpart
- confirming sponsorship or event participation
- sending sensitive or legally binding communications
- granting privileged system access
- representing a prospect, campaign, placement, booking or partnership as confirmed

The department may prepare, qualify, draft and stage these actions without representing them as completed.

## Full event-management checklist

Before execution, capture:

- event date/time/location
- venue and capacity
- production requirements
- suppliers/vendors
- staffing
- guest list process
- partner/sponsor commitments
- budget and payment gates
- insurance/legal requirements where applicable
- accessibility and safety requirements
- photography/video rights
- contingency plan
- communications/press plan
- creator/media accreditation process
- event-day owner and escalation path
- post-event reporting owner

## Department handoff model

**Creator → Campaign:** qualified creator + brief + deliverables + rights + schedule.

**Recruitment → Delivery:** approved role + candidate evidence + interview record + owner decision + onboarding handoff.

**PR → Coverage:** evidenced media target + story angle + approved assets + outreach record + response/coverage evidence.

**Events → Partnership:** evidenced event/partner + scope + commercial terms + production plan + approvals + counterpart confirmation.

**Post-event/campaign → Retention:** delivered assets + performance/coverage evidence + commercial outcome + lessons + next relationship action.
