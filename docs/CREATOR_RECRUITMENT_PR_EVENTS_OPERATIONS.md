# Creator, Recruitment, PR & Events Operations

Status: READY — operating contract added to the canonical MindReply product root. This document defines execution boundaries; it does not claim external partnerships, hires, placements, media coverage, event bookings, or live campaigns until evidence exists.

## Department mandate

The department coordinates four connected workstreams:

1. Content creator operations — briefs, creator discovery, qualification, deliverables, rights, publishing calendar and performance evidence.
2. Recruitment — role intake, candidate sourcing, screening, interview stages, offer/approval gates and onboarding handoff.
3. PR management — media/press targets, story angles, press assets, outreach staging, response tracking and coverage evidence.
4. Full event management partnerships — event discovery, venue/vendor/partner qualification, budgets, production plans, guest operations, sponsorships and post-event reporting.

## Operating pipeline

DISCOVER → QUALIFY → BRIEF → APPROVE → EXECUTE → EVIDENCE → REPORT → RETAIN

No external commitment is considered executed merely because a task, draft, repository record or proposal exists.

## Record types

Every operational item should carry:

- owner
- department
- brand/product
- counterpart or organisation
- objective
- stage
- next action
- due date
- budget, when applicable
- approval requirement
- evidence location
- status

Recommended stages:

INTAKE, RESEARCH, QUALIFIED, PROPOSAL, AWAITING_APPROVAL, COMMITTED, IN_EXECUTION, DELIVERED, EVIDENCE_REVIEW, CLOSED, DECLINED, BLOCKED.

## Evidence gates

A creator is qualified only when identity, audience/relevance, commercial fit and available evidence have been reviewed.

A recruitment candidate is qualified only when the role requirements and candidate evidence are recorded.

A PR opportunity is qualified only when the publication/contact opportunity and story relevance are evidenced.

An event partnership is qualified only when the event/partner, commercial terms and execution requirements are evidenced.

A partnership becomes committed only after the required owner approval and counterpart confirmation exist.

## Approval boundaries

Owner approval is required before:

- signing or accepting a contract
- committing spend
- publishing on behalf of a third party
- promising deliverables to an external counterpart
- confirming sponsorship or event participation
- sending sensitive or legally binding communications
- granting privileged system access

The department may prepare, qualify, draft and stage these actions without representing them as completed.

## Content and PR deliverables

Each campaign can produce:

- creative brief
- creator shortlist
- press angle and media list
- outreach draft
- asset checklist
- publishing schedule
- event run-of-show
- partner/sponsor matrix
- post-campaign evidence report

## Event management checklist

Before execution, capture:

- event date/time/location
- venue and capacity
- production requirements
- suppliers/vendors
- staffing
- guest list process
- partner/sponsor commitments
- budget and payment gates
- insurance/legal requirements where applicable
- accessibility and safety requirements
- photography/video rights
- contingency plan
- post-event reporting owner
