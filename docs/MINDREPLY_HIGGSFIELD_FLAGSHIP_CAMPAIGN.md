# MindReply Flagship Campaign — Production Brief

**Status:** READY for creative review; no media generation submitted  
**Owner:** MindReply owner  
**Production workspace:** [Higgsfield campaign canvas](https://higgsfield.ai/canvas/07617f56-ac1f-461b-80c5-b81c57cc5eda)

## Objective

Produce one premium, coherent campaign for MindReply that explains the value quickly and encourages qualified enquiries for the Website Completion Package (GBP 600), without promising unverified outcomes. The live offer, price and checkout path must be checked immediately before any campaign is published.

## Message

**MindReply — Think Clearly. Decide Fast. Move Forward.**

Support the message with a practical outcome: make a confusing digital or operational problem clearer, identify the next useful action, and help the customer move forward. Do not imply guaranteed savings, revenue, conversion, uptime, customer results or autonomous execution without evidence.

## Creative direction

- Premium editorial composition; generous whitespace and strong typographic hierarchy.
- Warm near-white surfaces, deep charcoal text, and a restrained accent.
- Calm, deliberate motion; clear subject and message in the opening seconds.
- Realistic, rights-safe visual language; avoid generic glowing brains, robot imagery, fake dashboards and fabricated customer testimonials.
- Keep the brand name, CTA and essential copy legible on mobile crops.
- Keep the public-facing language human and outcome-led; avoid unnecessary technical jargon.

## Proposed first production batch

1. **Hero key visual** — one master composition for campaign and landing-page use.
2. **Short product/service film** — a concise problem → clarity → next step narrative; final duration and aspect ratios to be chosen after checking the available Higgsfield models and actual quote.
3. **Three social adaptations** — derived from the approved master, not three unrelated concepts.
4. **Landing-page visual** — support the offer and a clear enquiry/checkout action.
5. **Copy pack** — headline, supporting line, CTA and short/long caption, with no unsupported claims.

## Workflow and approval

1. Validate the current public offer and target audience.
2. Review this brief and confirm the first visual direction.
3. Check available Higgsfield presets/models and obtain an actual generation quote.
4. Present settings, output count and total cost for approval **before** any paid generation.
5. Generate the first master asset only; review framing, copy legibility, consistency, rights and factual claims.
6. After approval, create the remaining adaptations and export the actual files.
7. Verify the final files and record their URLs/status. Publication, ad spend, customer contact and live-site changes require separate approval.

## Acceptance criteria

- One consistent visual system across all outputs.
- Mobile-safe framing and readable text.
- Accessible contrast and no meaning conveyed by color alone.
- No invented customers, testimonials, metrics or performance claims.
- Actual output/job status and exported asset evidence recorded.
- Actual spend recorded; no spend inferred from an estimate.
- Commercial performance evaluated separately using real enquiry, paid invoice/receipt, refund and operating-cost evidence.

## Evidence and status

At this stage, the campaign brief and Higgsfield canvas exist. No image/video generation has been submitted, no final media has been exported, and no campaign has been published. These states must remain distinct until supported by tool and production evidence.
