# Replycraft Contract

**Role:** customer-facing product core.

Replycraft is the language of useful interaction in this repository. It replaces generic assistant patterns with grounded, task-oriented communication.

## Experience principles

- **Human first:** begin from the person's situation, not the model.
- **Proof before promise:** show what happened or what remains unknown.
- **Small next move:** expose the safest useful action first.
- **No theatre:** no invented confidence, fake activity, fake autonomy or unsupported outcomes.
- **Recovery is a feature:** every meaningful mutation should have a visible recovery path.
- **Accessible by default:** keyboard, screen reader, reduced motion, contrast, touch targets and plain language are release requirements.

## Product vocabulary

- `Replycraft` — grounded response/action layer.
- `Reality Note` — evidence attached to a claim.
- `Soft Step` — reversible next action.
- `Open Loop` — work that is genuinely unfinished.
- `Return Path` — recovery/undo path.

Do not reuse these names as generic branding across unrelated repositories.
