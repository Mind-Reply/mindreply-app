## Research rules
- Each organisation below is a **market-map entity**, not a claimed prospect, customer, partner, or interested party.
- Public-source presence establishes only that the organisation and its stated activities exist. It does not establish buying intent, availability, capacity, a relationship, or commercial fit.
- Account qualification requires a separately documented use case, relevant buyer role, evidence of need, delivery fit, and a human-reviewed next step.
- Keep facts, analyst hypotheses, and unknowns in separate fields.
- Record the source publication/update date and the date checked.
- One accountable owner per account; owner assignment remains pending until an authorised team member is named.
