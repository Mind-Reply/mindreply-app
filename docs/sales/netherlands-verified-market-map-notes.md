## Evidence-backed organisation records

### 1. Groningen Seaports — Eemshaven / Delfzijl ecosystem
**Segment:** Port authority, industrial sites, energy and logistics ecosystem  
**Verified public facts:** Groningen Seaports describes itself as the port authority, commercial operator and developer for Delfzijl, Eemshaven and adjoining industrial parks. Its published focus includes green energy, biobased chemical industry and circular industry.  
**Research relevance:** Primary source for mapping the Eemshaven industrial and energy ecosystem, land-allocation process and local infrastructure stakeholders.  
**Next validation:** Identify the correct public process for site enquiries and obtain project-specific confirmation for plots, utilities, grid connection, water and fibre.  
**Source:** https://www.groningen-seaports.com/en/about-us/organisation-profile/  
**Source checked:** 2026-11-04  
**Evidence outcome:** VERIFIED — organisation role and published focus. Site availability and project feasibility remain CONTINUE.

### 2. TenneT — Dutch high-voltage transmission
**Segment:** Electricity transmission system operator  
**Verified public facts:** The Netherlands Enterprise Agency identifies TenneT as the national electricity transmission operator and distinguishes it from regional distribution system operators.
