# Profit Engine 2026

## Purpose
Operate marketing as a profit-allocation system rather than a traffic or vanity-metric system.

## Operating loop
DEMAND → CAPTURE → QUALIFY → CONVERT → DELIVER → RETAIN → EXPAND → MEASURE PROFIT

## Required economics
For every material campaign, offer, channel and customer cohort track:
- Revenue
- Contribution margin
- Fully loaded CAC
- LTV / expected customer value
- CAC payback
- Conversion rate
- Retention / expansion
- Delivery and fulfilment cost
- Source / attribution evidence
- Cash outcome

## 2026 measurement layer
Use first-party data wherever possible. Combine deterministic attribution with incrementality testing, causal inference, media-mix analysis and cohort economics where appropriate. Never treat platform-reported ROAS as the sole decision metric.

## Growth mechanisms
1. Capture existing demand before buying additional demand.
2. Recover missed, abandoned and dormant opportunities.
3. Improve offer architecture, pricing and bundles.
4. Build retention, reactivation, expansion and referral loops.
5. Measure creative fatigue and audience overlap.
6. Reallocate capital toward proven contribution, not clicks.
7. Preserve an evidence trail for every material experiment.

## A11pro components
- Profit Radar: channel, offer, cohort and contribution visibility.
- Revenue Recovery: missed enquiry, abandoned journey and dormant-customer recovery.
- Offer Engine: pricing, packaging and bundle experiments.
- Acquisition Engine: channel execution connected to commercial outcomes.
- Conversion Layer: problem → proof → offer → action → payment.
- Retention & Expansion: repeat purchase, cross-sell, upsell, referral.
- Capital Allocation: recommendation queue based on contribution and payback.
- Evidence Ledger: hypothesis → cost → result → margin impact → decision → proof.

## Release controls
No automatic paid-media spend, irreversible pricing change, live billing change or external commitment without owner approval.

## Success condition
A campaign is not considered successful because traffic, leads or platform ROAS increased. The system must establish whether incremental economic value increased after relevant costs and whether the result is sufficiently evidenced to justify continued capital allocation.
