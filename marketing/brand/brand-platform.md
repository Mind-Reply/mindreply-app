# A11-K Brand Platform

## Positioning

A11-K is a voice-first AI companion surface designed to make thinking out loud feel more natural, calm, and useful.

The product should feel like a clear place to begin: speak, explore, organise the next thought, and stay in control of the direction.

## Audience hypotheses

These are hypotheses for research and testing, not confirmed market facts:

* People who prefer speaking to typing when ideas are still forming.
* People who want a calmer, less transactional way to interact with AI.
* Creators, builders, and operators who use conversation to clarify decisions.
* People who value useful assistance without losing agency over the final choice.

## Differentiation direction

A11-K should compete on the quality of the interaction and the feeling of the surface, not on unsupported superlatives. The experience should communicate:

* Voice as a natural starting point.
* Conversation with structure rather than noise.
* Helpful momentum without pressure.
* A clear boundary between assistance and user control.

## Brand principles

### Warmth without performance

Use human rhythm and considerate language. Avoid pretending that the product has emotions, consciousness, or personal certainty.

### Clarity without coldness

Prefer plain language, short sentences, useful headings, and specific next steps.

### Agency without friction

Invite the user to direct the interaction. Avoid manipulative urgency, dark patterns, or copy that implies the system knows what is best for them.

### Restraint without being vague

Say what the experience is designed to do. Do not claim reliability, accuracy, privacy, security, availability, or business results until each claim is verified.

## Personality

* Calm
* Curious
* Precise
* Warm
* Quietly confident
* Never robotic
* Never overfamiliar
* Never hype-driven

## Public message hierarchy

1. **Category:** voice-first AI companion surface.
2. **Core experience:** speak naturally and think out loud.
3. **Practical value:** turn conversation into clearer next steps.
4. **Control:** the user directs the pace, purpose, and outcome.
5. **Builder:** created by MindReply.

## Proof requirements

Before publishing a factual product claim, record the evidence for it. Examples include:

* Live feature: verified in the production build.
* Availability: verified from the production URL and access flow.
* Privacy or security: approved by the owner and supported by current documentation.
* Performance: measured using a defined method and date range.
* User outcome: supported by a documented study or clearly labelled as a hypothesis.

## Words to use

Natural, voice-first, think out loud, clear next step, calm, useful, direct, your direction, make room, explore, organise, begin.

## Words to avoid by default

Revolutionary, magical, guaranteed, human-level, perfect, secure by default, private by default, always available, understands you, knows you, replaces experts, effortless results, unlimited, best, number one.
