# A11-K Messaging System

## Recommended brand line

**Speak naturally. Think clearly.**

This is a direction for the brand, not a performance guarantee.

## Supporting lines

* An easier way to think out loud.
* Voice, with room to think.
* Start with a thought. Find the next step.
* A calmer surface for useful AI interaction.
* Let the conversation take shape.

## One-line description

A11-K is a voice-first AI companion surface designed for natural conversation, exploration, and clearer next steps.

## Short description

Speak naturally, explore an idea, and shape the next step with A11-K — a voice-first companion surface by MindReply.

## Longer description

A11-K gives conversation a calmer place to begin. Use your voice to explore an idea, work through a question, or organise what comes next. The direction remains yours; A11-K is designed to help the conversation take shape.

## Audience-specific angles

### Thinkers and planners

Turn a rough thought into a clearer next step.

### Creators and builders

Use conversation to explore, shape, and move an idea forward.

### Voice-first users

Begin with your voice instead of waiting for the perfect sentence.

### Teams and operators

Use a calmer starting point for questions, decisions, and working notes. Confirm team features before publishing team-oriented copy.

## Message rules

* Lead with the user's experience, not model specifications.
* Use “designed to” when describing intended value.
* Make access and availability explicit.
* Keep the user in control of the decision and the next step.
* Separate product facts from creative language.
* Do not imply clinical, legal, financial, or safety advice.
