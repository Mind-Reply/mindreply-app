# Launch Campaign Brief: The Natural Interface

## Status

Creative and copy foundation only. No spend or public activation is implied.

## Campaign idea

**The Natural Interface** presents voice as a natural starting point for thinking, exploring, and finding the next step.

The campaign should feel editorial and composed rather than loud or futuristic.

## Objective

Validate which message and creative territory creates qualified interest in A11-K before scaling distribution.

## Audience hypotheses

* People who think by speaking.
* People who want to explore ideas before committing to a polished prompt.
* Creators, builders, and operators who value clear next steps.
* Early adopters interested in a calmer AI interaction model.

Test these hypotheses; do not treat them as established audience facts.

## Creative territories

### Voice as thinking

Show the first rough thought as a valid beginning.

**Visual:** a single line or waveform moving from an unformed point into a clear shape.

### Calm by design

Position the surface as a quiet place to focus.

**Visual:** obsidian space, a small blue point of attention, short copy, and generous margins.

### Make room for the next thought

Focus on momentum without pressure.

**Visual:** a sequence of three minimal cards: speak, explore, next step.

## Channel sequence

| Stage | Channels | Asset | Primary signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learn | Organic social, founder or brand posts, communities | Short concept posts and static cards | Saves, replies, qualified visits |
| Test | Search, paid social, retargeting after consent | Copy and creative variants | Landing-page engagement and access intent |
| Scale | Best-performing approved channels | Adapted campaign kit | Qualified acquisition and activation |

## Asset matrix

* Landing page: one responsive concept and one production-approved version.
* Social: square, portrait, and landscape variants.
* Search: headline and description combinations.
* Short video: 10–15 second motion concept with captions and no required audio.
* Email: launch note, access confirmation, and follow-up sequence after the destination exists.

## Launch gates

* Product experience and access model confirmed.
* Public claims reviewed against evidence.
* Landing page passes accessibility and responsive checks.
* Signup or access route is functional.
* Privacy and consent language is available where data is collected.
* Analytics events are tested without sensitive payloads.
* UTMs and destination URLs are verified.
* Budget, owner, stop conditions, and reporting cadence are documented.

## Success definition

Start with learning quality, not vanity reach. A useful first test identifies:

* Which audience language creates relevant interest.
* Which creative territory earns meaningful engagement.
* Whether the landing page explains the product without overclaiming.
* Where users stop before the verified access action.
