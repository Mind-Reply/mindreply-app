# Four-Week Content Calendar

**Status:** Planning baseline. Dates and channels should be adjusted after the production launch date is known.

| Week | Theme | Assets | Channels | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The first thought | Static concept card, short post, landing-page preview | Organic social, community, owned site | Explore the concept |
| 2 | Voice as thinking | Short captioned motion concept, practical article brief | Social, blog, newsletter draft | See the experience |
| 3 | Calm interaction | Principle card set, founder or brand note, FAQ draft | Social, newsletter draft, community | Get launch updates |
| 4 | Launch readiness | Access announcement, search copy, retargeting variants | Owned channels, approved paid tests | Verified access CTA |

## Reusable weekly rhythm

* Monday: publish one useful idea, not a product claim.
* Tuesday: collect audience language and questions.
* Wednesday: test one visual or copy variation.
* Thursday: improve the landing experience from observed friction.
* Friday: record results, decisions, and next experiments.

## Content rules

* Every post has one purpose and one next step.
* Label concept content as concept content.
* Never imply that a preview is the production service.
* Avoid exposing private owner, infrastructure, or credential information.
* Keep claims consistent across ads, landing pages, social, and search metadata.
