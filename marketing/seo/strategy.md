# A11-K SEO Strategy

## SEO status

Prepare the structure now; keep the concept preview out of search until the production page, access flow, and public claims are ready.

## Search intent clusters

| Cluster | Intent | Content direction |
|---|---|---|
| Voice-first AI | Learn and compare | Explain the interaction model and show the concept honestly |
| AI voice companion | Discover | Describe who the experience is designed for and how access works |
| Think out loud with AI | Solve a task | Publish a practical guide with examples that do not imply guaranteed results |
| Natural AI interaction | Explore a category | Publish principles, use cases, and product context |
| A11-K MindReply | Navigate | Brand page, product overview, and verified access route |

## Initial keyword directions

Treat these as research starting points, not guaranteed-volume terms:

* voice-first AI
* AI voice companion
* think out loud with AI
* natural voice AI interaction
* AI companion for ideas
* conversational AI for brainstorming
* voice interface for thinking

Validate intent, competition, regional language, and search demand before allocating production content.

## Metadata direction

**Title:** A11-K | Speak naturally. Think clearly.  
**Description:** Explore A11-K, a voice-first AI companion surface designed for natural conversation, clearer thinking, and useful next steps.

Use this only when the destination accurately represents the live experience.

## Technical checklist

* One canonical production URL.
* One descriptive H1 per page.
* Unique title and meta description per indexable page.
* Open Graph and social image with accurate copy.
* `robots.txt` and sitemap generated from the verified production routes.
* Noindex on previews, staging, and incomplete access pages.
* Descriptive image filenames and alt text.
* Internal links between product, principles, use cases, and access pages.
* JSON-LD only for facts that are true on the production page.
* Fast loading, responsive layout, keyboard access, and visible focus.
* No private owner data, credentials, internal URLs, or diagnostic output in indexable content.

## Content quality standard

Each article should answer one clear user intent, use original examples, distinguish experience design from product fact, and provide a next step. Avoid thin pages made only to target a keyword.

## Publishing gates

Do not request indexing until:

1. The production URL resolves correctly.
2. The page is not a preview or placeholder.
3. Access and contact routes work.
4. Metadata and structured data match the page.
5. The owner has reviewed public claims.
