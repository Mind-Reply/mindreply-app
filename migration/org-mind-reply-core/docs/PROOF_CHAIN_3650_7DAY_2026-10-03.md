## 4. Revenue and net model

Gross = (billable_replies × price_per_billable) + (paying_licenses × $49)

Net = Gross - infra_excess - Stripe - support - CAC - tax - refunds/chargebacks

The $0.001 reply price is a planning/product assumption until an actual billing contract and transaction evidence establish it.

Micro-charges must be bundled. Do not create one Stripe charge/invoice per $0.001 event. Monthly aggregation is the intended billing model.
