# Programmatic SEO

## Core rule
Every generated page must provide genuine, useful, differentiated value. Never publish thin pages that merely swap a keyword, industry, competitor, tool, or term.

## Playbooks
### Personas
For patterns such as CRM for [industry], research intent and require industry-specific data, workflows, pain points, examples, terminology, and product value. Use clean URLs such as /industries/real-estate and connect pages through an industry hub and contextual links.

### Comparisons
For [A] vs [B], research each query and intent. Include comparison-specific evidence, capabilities, workflows, trade-offs, migration considerations, and only verifiable pricing. Prefer /compare/a-vs-b. Cross-reference the competitors playbook when available.

### Integrations
For [tool] integration, include what it does, setup, supported workflows, use cases, limitations, troubleshooting, and concrete integration-specific evidence. Prefer /integrations/tool and provide an integrations hub.

### Glossary
For [term] definition, include a useful definition, examples, related concepts, practical application, and product relevance where appropriate. Prefer /glossary/term, with alphabetical/categorical hub navigation and DefinedTerm structured data when valid.

## Indexation gates
Verify unique substantial content, search-intent alignment, contextual internal links, XML sitemap inclusion, robots/meta-robots directives, canonicals, duplicate risk, crawl efficiency, and intentional handling of low-value pages. Do not assume crawl budget is the cause of non-indexation.

## Technical boundary
For crawl errors, 404s, server responses, robots directives, canonicals, or other implementation defects, use/cross-reference seo-audit.

## Output
Return page model, unique data requirements, keyword/intent approach, URL pattern, internal linking, indexation strategy, and quality gates.
